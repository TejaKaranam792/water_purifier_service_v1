Our Story & Commitment to Visakhapatnam Families

Founded to bridge the gap between expensive brand service centers and unreliable local handymen, HMT Aqua has grown to serve residential homes, apartments, clinics, and businesses throughout Visakhapatnam. We believe clean, bacteria-free water is a fundamental right, not an expensive luxury. By maintaining a network of mobile technicians equipped with genuine parts and calibrated digital TDS meters, we reach any doorstep in Vizag within 30 to 60 minutes.
